How to serve

Three steps, thirty seconds

01

Warm the sachet. Stand it in hot water for two minutes — never microwave.

02

Pour over food or serve neat. Half a sachet for cats and small dogs, a full sachet above 10kg.

03

Repeat daily. Once a day for maintenance, twice during recovery or after surgery.
